how it works

Three views. No clutter.

01

Round timer

Configurable rolling and rest intervals. Loud haptic at round end. Auto-advance through rounds. Big clock you can read across the gym.

02

Technique library

Filter by category, belt level, and position. Each entry has a diagram, the cue, and the most common mistake. Drill them in sets.

03

Session log

What got you tapped, what worked, what to drill next. Coverage bars show weak positions. No streak shaming.

faq

Quick answers.

Do I need an account?

No. The app stores everything in your browser. Closing the tab doesn't lose your session log — it just keeps it local.

Gi or no-gi?

Both. The technique library is tagged for gi and no-gi, and the timer works the same for either.

Can I customize the round / rest length?

Yes — defaults are 5/2 minutes, 5 rounds, but you can change them per session. Comp sims are 6 rounds with 1-minute rest.

Is this for competition prep?

It's a session tool, not a curriculum. Pair it with your coach's programming and it'll track what you did and what you got hit by.

Why bento?

Because the mat has zones. A live timer, a session plan, a technique library, and a coverage log — each one a tile, each one readable from across the gym.
